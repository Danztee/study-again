# Content credits

The three curated fractions lessons in Study Again adapt ideas from OpenStax, *Prealgebra 2e*, published by Rice University. The adapted educational content is attributed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). This proof of concept is non-commercial.

- [Section 4.4: Add and Subtract Fractions with Common Denominators](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/4-4-add-and-subtract-fractions-with-common-denominators)
- [Section 4.5: Add and Subtract Fractions with Different Denominators](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/4-5-add-and-subtract-fractions-with-different-denominators)
- [Section 4.2: Multiply and Divide Fractions](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/4-2-multiply-and-divide-fractions)

The interface and practice questions were written for this prototype. Learner-added notes and links belong to the learner and remain in their browser.
